## English

**[Download Dynamic-Delay-Setup.exe](https://github.com/ragnarcb/obs-dynamic-delay/releases/latest/download/Dynamic-Delay-Setup.exe)** · English, Portuguese and Spanish in one file

**The on-screen widget now shows up while you are not live**, with your set delay, so you can place and style it in OBS before the stream. Turn off "Show it while not live too" in its block if you record offline.

**Auto-off timer and your own delay buttons.** Pick "Turn off by itself" (15 to 120 min) in the Delay block, or type `!delay 60 20m` in chat, so the delay is not left on after the ranked match. "Edit the delay buttons" takes up to 6 values (tournaments often ask for 120 to 300 s) and shows the memory they need.

**Spanish, Kick fix and more.** Spanish is now a third language everywhere. Saving Settings no longer replaces a custom Kick ingest address, a destination with a slow connection no longer grows memory without limit, stalled connections reconnect in 15 s, and bots such as Streamer.bot can listen to live events at `/api/events`. Join the community on Discord: https://discord.gg/crctbnQ2f8

To update: run the new Setup, it installs over the old version and keeps your settings. Full list in the [changelog](https://github.com/ragnarcb/obs-dynamic-delay/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
